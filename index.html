4 Clipchamp Workflow

A browser page cannot reach into Clipchamp's editor and drive it automatically — that's a normal browser security boundary, not a bug in this tool. Here's what actually happens on each side.

Handled automatically by this page

  • Splitting your script into parts
  • Copying a part to your clipboard
  • Tracking progress across parts
  • Organizing your downloaded audio files
  • Reordering and merging that audio
  • Exporting the final file — all on your device

Not possible from a static page

  • Opening Clipchamp's editor for you
  • Clicking Clipchamp's Generate button
  • Downloading Clipchamp's output automatically
  1. Copy Part 1 using the Copy button above.
  2. Click Open Clipchamp to open it in a new tab.
  3. Paste the text into Clipchamp's Text to Speech tool.
  4. Generate the speech in Clipchamp.
  5. Download / export the generated audio clip from Clipchamp.
  6. Return to this tab.
  7. Click Mark audio ready on Part 1.
  8. Continue with Part 2, and so on.

    6 Privacy

    Your text and audio files are processed locally in your browser. This website does not upload your script or audio files to a server.

    No analytics, no cookies, no tracking scripts, and no login. This page loads two small open-source libraries from a public CDN (JSZip for .zip downloads, and lamejs only if you choose MP3 export) — your content is never sent to them or anywhere else.
